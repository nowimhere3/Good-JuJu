GROW YOUR PRACTICE

Add to your collection

Paste copied Google Docs text or open an exported .txt file. Start each card with a title, then its description. Separate cards with ========== on its own line. Paragraphs are preserved.

Select a whole batch of image pairs: F1 + B1, F2 + B2, and so on. Names and extensions are case insensitive. JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP and AVIF are supported. Each two-page PDF is one card: page 1 front, page 2 back. PDF limit: 30 MB per file; batch limit: 120 MB. Convert TIFF scans to PNG first. Existing cards are never overwritten.

KEEP YOUR COLLECTION CLOSE

Backup & restore

Your browser stores your personal library independently. Export regularly, especially before changing devices or clearing browser data.

Backups include your digital cards, both faces of personal image cards, favorites, tags, archive status and personal edits. R1 images stay in the packaged master library.

Merge keeps other cards. For matching identities, values in the backup take priority. No cards are deleted.

Rename deck

This changes the name in this browser only. It does not rename a Windows folder or change the deck's permanent identity.
